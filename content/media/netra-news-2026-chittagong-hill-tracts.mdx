In this interview with Netra News (August 2026), I discussed the ongoing armed conflict between the JSS and the UPDF in the Chittagong Hill Tracts and what it means for the indigenous communities living there. Drawing on my research on territorial autonomy and post-accord settlements, I argued that the autonomy arrangements of the 1997 peace accord have not resolved the underlying conflict over self-determination, and that fragmentation among self-determination movements can sustain violence long after a settlement has been signed. The interview was published as an article and as a video report on the Netra News YouTube channel.
