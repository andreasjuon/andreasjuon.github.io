I am teaching this MA seminar at the University of Fribourg from September 2026. The course introduces students to theories and empirical research on why political violence breaks out and how it escalates, de-escalates, and ends. We cover civil war, ethnic conflict, repression, and communal violence, and we examine the institutional and structural conditions under which grievances translate into organized armed conflict.
